# Diana Clum — Character Card

Diana Clum lost her tail under unknown circumstances. To atone for her guilt, instead of entering the Encapsulated dimension, she volunteered to destroy all remaining Resonant Charoites and Melodyonators located in dimension 1A1-66-115. However, one Melodyonator ended up under heavy security in the Galactic Museum of Technology under the Eidolian Defense Forces (GMT under EDF). Another one was hidden on the planet Aciwamp by Sergey Hepunkt.

At a certain point, the deployment of ISORI and Breguet into the Living World from the Chord dimension occurred. Naturally, such a highly specific event drew attention, and pirates began looting ISORI, precisely as Breguet had anticipated. Meanwhile, Breguet simply hid away in the middle of nowhere, naming this place the Gahvarian Nebula.

Diana found out about this strange computer. ISORI would occasionally tell the pirates bizarre things and allow them to download events that existed in the Chord world. She went to ask if it knew how to infiltrate the GMT. Logically, ISORI asked why she needed this. Diana then explained her mission: the Antechent must under no circumstances enter dimension 2A1-66-115, where the Shrampite Empire triumphed in the Great Shrampite Conquista. And vice versa—the Empire must not enter this dimension and find the Antechent. ISORI contacted Breguet, who provided information that could be shared with Diana. ISORI replied that she shouldn't worry about this particular Melodyonator—the Antechent knows nothing about it. However, another Melodyonator is hidden on Aciwamp, and there is one more planet in the Tonata galaxy that serves as a source of Resonant Charoite. In exchange, ISORI asked Diana to keep an eye out for creatures with a black beard, glasses, and blue skin —Crutches— if she ever encounters them.

Diana found this Melodyonator and used it to travel to Tonata. There, she convinced Quand to destroy the Harmony Quite using the Acatadop and to utilize it for Quand's own custom-made planet — Wealth Refussi. Afterward, she saw a TV report featuring a creature that matched ISORI's description giving an interview. The creature stated: "Allemauses in 11/F17-99 might interfere with drowning out the melody! Do not listen to creatures in green suits!" Following this, the scene abruptly cut off. Naturally, everyone at the news agency panicked when Unlas's interview (and he was wearing a green suit, which was the signature color of the Tonata Rangers) was suddenly interrupted... by this. But nobody understood a thing.

However, Diana understood and headed to the Ellesmere galaxy to begin a rebel struggle against Alleonzern. At a certain point, driven by completely separate events, the escaped Boris Hartocat and Witya Peros — who was chasing him — arrive in that same galaxy. They engage in a high-speed shootout right on the city streets. One of them, Boris, is just a criminal, while the second, Witya, has simply gotten used to impunity within the ranks of the Tonata Rangers. Naturally, that didn't fly here, and both ended up in prison.

Both were hired by Alleonzern to stop Diana and Merc-4-more. However, after the first battle against Diana, Boris escapes, kidnaps Peter Koen (the head of Alleonzern), and disguises himself as him in order to put the Allemauses into production due to their propensity for violence. His plan was to later defeat them himself, become a hero, and stop being a criminal. Yet, during their next encounter, Diana manages to get through to Witya, and he defects to her side. They travel and fight together from then on. Throughout their joint adventures, Merc-4-more betrays the heroes for a more lucrative contract, and Diana ends up trapped inside the chest of a giant mech, which Witya, fortunately, manages to destroy without harming Diana.

Soon, she finds exactly what ISORI sent her to Ellesmere for: an ideal, paired shard of Resonant Charoite located in the Alleonzern Geological Center. Diana unpairs this shard and inserts it into her Melodyonator, making its operation far more stable and safe.

After the victory over Alleonzern, Witya, appealing to the fact that he rescued her from the mech, convinces Diana to return with him to the Tonata Galaxy. This is because, while they were away, the Tonata government declared war on the Nanomites following the incident with Boris. At that exact moment, the Terrelipuses stabbed them in the back, bribed by Sience, who, after his robotization, became obsessed with the idea of turning everyone into robots. Diana and Diana together, utilizing the Melodyonator, return to Tonata and take part in the events of HoS!

Diana will not be on Bennu 1 during the gas attack, due to which Witya is put to sleep, taken to the Cooloseum station, and fitted with an explosive collar. As a result, he is forced to fight there side by side with Doctor Sience.

Diana will go after him when he is captured by the Platifichs to be delivered to the Antechent. However, she does not make contact but observes from the sidelines, as she is unaware of the events of DoC and CiF and ends up shocked to discover that there are now two Wityas: Witya and his Clone.

Seeing that the Clone simply left for a dimension the Antechent designated as the "Fustix dimension," she leaps after him, knowing full well that the real Fustix dimension is encapsulated and opening a rift into it is impossible. Because of this, during the subsequent events of NoA, Witya spends his time fighting side by side with Dina Hepunkt. Parallel to these adventures, ISORI turns out to be repaired, though this was all according to Breguet's plan.

Shortly before the events of EoS, a dispute occurs between Breguet and the repaired ISORI. According to the "script," a certain character — currently nameless — was supposed to enter dimension 2A1-66-115 with his knowledge of the Chords, and such a scenario threatened the very existence of the Chords themselves. Breguet asked ISORI for help in order to rewrite the script, and ISORI agreed, providing an alternative variant. In this version, Alarein Suunt learns about the existence of the Great Breguet and rewinds time so as not to trust the Antechent. The operator turns out to be immune to the time rewind and wouldn't forget, allowing him to set up the events differently. After some time, ISORI realized that rewriting the script would not solve the problem with dimension 2A1-66-115, where the Shrampite Empire triumphed. Defending the existence of the Chords, its own creators, from the unstoppable threat of the victorious Shrampite Empire to the entire Living World was a task simpler to resolve elsewhere. Ultimately, it came to Katya in the guise of Crutch with a mustache, beard, and glasses, but wearing a Green Suit, and gave her a clue about seven minutes.

By the end of the events of EoS, Diana and the Clone returned to dimension 1A1-66-115, found out Witya's location, and reached the Gahvarian Nebula at the exact moment when Alarein Suunt killed Witya, precisely as Breguet had planned.

However, Katya had already run away, which was not part of Breguet's plan, and she failed to talk Diana and the Clone out of attacking Alarein. Naturally, upon seeing Witya's corpse and an evil Alarein... they asked very few questions. Breguet realized that if Suunt were killed, it would automatically trigger the execution of the bad scenario. Breguet then orchestrated the deaths of Partys Solis and Jord Solis so that the script would still have to be rewritten regardless.

But then Katya rewound time by 7 minutes, and all the dead were resurrected. Due to "phantom pain," Jord took an extra step, causing a Fishbeast to bite off his tail instead of his liver; Partys Solis dropped a crate, which crushed the very piece of slime she had previously slipped on and fallen into the acid swamp; Witya dropped to his knees due to a phantom sensation of a fatal gunshot, causing Alarein to miss. As a result, when the Clone and Diana arrived again in the alternative timeline, Witya talked Diana and the Clone out of attacking Alarein and explained that he was simply trying to leave, because Alarein doesn't listen to anyone and will destroy the entire creation this way. In response, Suunt announces that he wants to undo his mistake. Consequently, when Katya returns, she sees this group of four but doesn't understand what she is supposed to do. Katya knows that Breguet is an empathetic-less executioner of scripts; she knows her own role as a module for Breguet that is supposed to teach him empathy, but Katya completely fails to comprehend how Breguet allowed Alarein to get here. Whiteman initiated her into the main script, but she knows nothing about the "variation" created by ISORI.

Suunt says, look, she rewound time! The Clone and Diana simply have no clue what is even going on. Meanwhile, Witya continues to assert that this will destroy the creation. Like, 7 minutes and several decades are completely different things. Kat refrains from answering. She doesn't understand what is happening either—this game played by two Supercomputers, the conductors of the Living World. Ultimately, the entire group proceeds to the hall. Alarein begins the rewind, but this time the rollback fails to run stably. Everything would have been fine, time would have rewound just as Breguet had planned, but Witya is alive. He keeps nagging everyone. His crew is on his side. To the Clone, Alarein is just a stranger. But Diana knows him. She also knows about Darla Suunt, about his daughter. Yet she remains silent. Alarein himself realizes that "Witya's crew" will believe Witya rather than him. In the end, Alarein snaps, accepts the version that this will destroy the creation, and sacrifices himself, while simultaneously disabling the Great Breguet. The Clone wants to sacrifice himself instead, but Diana stops him and straight up tells him to cut it out with his imposter syndrome.

Diana and the Clone do not take part in the events of ---name missing for now--- because the Clone receives a message from Crutch stating that "one of the creatures with blue skin and blue blood got into big trouble in ---name missing for now--- in 1H7-47-298, access in shoe covers." The Clone and Witya agree that the Clone will leave with Diana, while Witya stays behind to rest.

Diana and the Clone, in their thief guises (wearing shoe covers) distinguished by their eye color, fail to find any blue-skinned creature in ---name missing for now---. Instead, they find Partys Solis there—a Fustix with blue fur—and break her out of prison. Partys refuses to go with them, and they, in turn, refuse to help Partys against Emperor Sience, saying, look, we weren't even looking for you here anyway. Following this, Diana asks the Clone point-blank if that creature was in a green suit, to which the Clone replies yes, it was. Angela states that creatures like that cannot be trusted, and they head back into dimension 1A1-66-115.

In the events of FaN, the Clone and Witya take turns participating, while Witya finally gets to rest. Diana is constantly present in the events to support the CO-OP mode. The events of this game remain shrouded in mystery for me for now. What I know for certain is that Witya will tell her about the Symphony Shard, which he used to banish the Antechent to dimension 2A1-66-115, a revelation that will severely ruin her mood. However, she will not disclose the reason.

At the beginning of SoO, Kat restores the Melodyonator but makes a mistake with the timeline, which is why the Clone, rather than Witya, steps out to host the parade. Doctor Sience also arrives at the parade in order to use the Melodyonator to enter a dimension where he always wins. A battle erupts over the Melodyonator, but Witya and Diana fail to hear it. Witya is wearing headphones, listening to the song "Fragments," while Diana is cooking food for the Allemauses in a thoroughly soundproofed kitchen.

At the beginning of RoM, Witya and the Clone swap places. They play Rock-Paper-Scissors, a game from the Chord world that Kat told them about. The Clone loses, meaning the real Witya is the one dispatched to the Fustix dimension. Diana remains in this dimension to keep an eye on things, stating that they aren't exactly thrilled to see her there. Witya asks why but receives no clear answer. Soon, the Chords arrive to meet the Clone and Diana, begging them for help in protecting their dimension, which has become the target of the main assault by the Shrampite Empire under the leadership of ---name missing for now---. Meanwhile, the Antechent has led a portion of his forces to attack the Fustix dimension. It is precisely during the events of RoM that Witya discovers what Diana Clum's actual mission was, why she stayed in this dimension, and why she failed this mission. After all, the Antechent did manage to enter dimension 2A1-66-115.

The events of DeF pass Diana by; she and the clone are fully occupied with defending the Chord dimension, exactly as they are during the events of EvE.

Diana takes a direct part in the events of GoD, fighting against the widespread breach of the Nether, and stands alongside everyone else in the final battle when God Partys arrives and kills the last, perfect architect of the Nether—Skad Peros, Witya's real father, corrupted by the Nether.

With this, the story of not only Diana but the entire Living World comes to an end.

Moving forward, a portion of the events is undone; the path fades into the infinity of fan fiction. It is no longer up to me to decide what is canon and what is not. The Living World truly becomes Living.
